Hide an antimatter domain

Suppose the universe were a patchwork of matter and antimatter regions. After recombination the regions touch, gas flows into every boundary and annihilates, and the gamma rays from 1100 > 1 + z > 20 would glow across today's sky at 1–45 MeV. Make the domains large enough and the glow fades below what COMPTEL measured. How large is large enough?
