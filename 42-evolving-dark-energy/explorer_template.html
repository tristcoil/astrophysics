Profile likelihoods computed by 42-evolving-dark-energy/explorer.py with this project's own likelihood, which reproduces DESI's published best-fit χ² values. The compressed CMB prior (Lemos & Lewis 2023, as used by DESI) carries less information than the full CMB likelihood with lensing, so significances here are lower than DESI's headline 2.8–4.2σ. w0–wa is a convenient parametrization, not a physical model.
