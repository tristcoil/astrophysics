Find the echo

The disk of hot gas around a black hole flickers. Clouds of gas farther out light up in the broad emission lines when the flicker reaches them, a little later. Slide the line's light curve back in time until it matches the continuum: the shift is the light-travel time to the clouds, and with the speed of the clouds (the line's width) it weighs the black hole.

Table view of the data
Data: Pei et al. 2017 (NGC 5548, 2014); Peterson et al. 2013 (NGC 5548, 1988-2008); Shen et al. 2024 (SDSS-RM). Both curves are scaled to zero mean and unit variance so their shapes can be compared. The correlation curve is this project's interpolated cross-correlation function; the measured lag is the median of 500-1000 resamplings of the data. Mass = f c τ σ² / G with log f = 0.63 (dynamical models; uncertain by a factor of 2 for any single AGN).
