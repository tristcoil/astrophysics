Build a supernova's light from nickel

Explosions forge radioactive nickel-56, which decays to cobalt-56 and then to iron. The gamma rays from those decays heat the debris, and that heat is the light we see for months. Choose a supernova and set how much nickel it made: the model below is the decay chain, plus light diffusing out of the debris, plus gamma rays escaping once the debris thins.
