Weigh the M-σ relation yourself

Every point is a galaxy whose central black hole has been weighed. Heavier black holes sit in galaxies whose stars move faster (larger velocity dispersion σ), but the slope of that trend depends on which galaxies you count. Switch kinds of galaxies in and out and the line is refitted here, in your browser.

Data: van den Bosch 2016 (ApJ 831, 134; author's GitHub table), Kormendy & Ho 2013 (ARA&A 51, 511; Tables 2-3 from the arXiv source), McConnell & Ma 2013 (ApJ 764, 184; Table A1). Milky Way: this atlas's project H1; M87*: EHT Collaboration 2019. Fit: maximum likelihood with intrinsic scatter in log M, errors in both axes, the distribution of true σ modelled as a Gaussian (Kelly 2007), upper limits censored; ± from the curvature of the likelihood.
