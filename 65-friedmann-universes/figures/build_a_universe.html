What the measurements say

The Friedmann equation is solved exactly in the page itself (time integrals with the turning points removed by substitution; the same method as the project's Python code, checked there against closed-form solutions, a direct integration of the acceleration equation, and CAMB). Probability regions (68% dark, 95% light) are our own fits with curvature free: Pantheon+ supernovae (Scolnic et al. 2022), DESI DR2 BAO (2025) and the compressed Planck PR4 CMB (Lemos & Lewis 2023). Speedometer data: Moresco et al. (2022) cosmic chronometers (diagonal errors, drawn only) and DESI DR2 line-of-sight BAO converted with our rd.
