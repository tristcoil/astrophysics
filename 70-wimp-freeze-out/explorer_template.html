The race

Each WIMP meets a partner at a rate n⟨σv⟩, while the universe expands at a rate H. Early on, collisions win and the abundance tracks equilibrium, which falls as e−m/T. At x = m/T ≈ 20 the dwindling WIMPs can no longer find each other, and what is left is frozen in. Twice the cross section leaves about half as much.

What the numbers come from

The plasma's degrees of freedom g*(T) are Saikawa & Shirai's (2020), lattice QCD included. The project's solver reproduces their published relic cross sections to 0.1%. Here the same implicit scheme runs with 300 steps per e-fold, so the page agrees with the project to about 0.02%. The target is Planck's Ωch² = 0.120.

What this leaves out

One particle species, annihilating to Standard Model particles that stay in thermal equilibrium. Co-annihilation with a heavier partner, resonances, Sommerfeld enhancement, or dark matter made another way (freeze-in, axions) change the story. A p-wave WIMP annihilates a million times more slowly today, so dwarf galaxies cannot test it.
