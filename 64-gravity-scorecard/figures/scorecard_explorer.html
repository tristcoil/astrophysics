Is Einstein still right?

46 tests of general relativity, from laboratory balances to black holes. Each point is one test, placed by the 1σ uncertainty of its measured deviation from GR (or of an effect GR forbids) against the strength of the gravity it probes, GM/(rc²). Stars are measurements made in this atlas. Hover over or tap a point for its source.

Values were checked against the primary papers (literature/checks/*.csv in project N7 of the atlas) and converted to 1σ: 90% limits ÷ 1.645, 95% ÷ 1.96, probable errors × 1.4826, statistical and systematic errors added in quadrature. GM/(rc²) is a characteristic value for each test, not a unique number. Overlapping points are nudged sideways; the tooltips and the table give the exact values. In the report card, if GR is right and the errors are honest, about 68% of tests should land inside ±1σ and 95% inside ±2σ.
