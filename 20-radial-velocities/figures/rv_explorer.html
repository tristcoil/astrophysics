Fold it yourself

A planet makes its star's velocity repeat every orbit. Pick a star, then click a peak in the periodogram (or a suggestion) to fold the velocities on that period. The right period stacks every orbit into one clean curve; an alias, produced by observing only at night, leaves a mess.
